## Full description
Meet your slimes. Splice their genes. Evolve something never seen before.

Slimeverse is a deep idle monster-breeding RPG where every slime is a unique genetic experiment. Collect adorable, expressive slimes across ten elements and sixteen families, then fuse, breed and mutate them to discover hundreds of spectacular evolutions — including rare Prismatic shinies and four secret hybrid families.

GENETICS THAT MATTER
Every slime carries eight genes, each with a dominant and a recessive allele. Dominant traits shape its body, eyes, crown and aura; recessive traits can surface in offspring. Breed Mendelian-style, fuse hybrid species, or spend mutagen to reroll a gene. Thousands of meaningful combinations — not just recolors.

BUILD YOUR SQUAD
Form a five-slime squad with a front and back row. Trigger element resonances, family bonds and trait synergies. Save loadout presets and let smart automation pick your best team.

AUTO-BATTLE, DEEP STRATEGY
Battles run automatically with satisfying VFX, crits, shields, heals, stuns and damage-over-time. Master elemental affinities, skill cooldowns and passive traits to punch far above your power.

ENDLESS THINGS TO DO
• 12 themed zones with bosses and auto-progress
• An endless Tower with milestone rewards
• Daily dungeons for essence, mutagen and gold
• Timed expeditions that reward you even offline
• Four living habitats that produce resources while you rest
• A 24-node research lab and element mastery trees
• 60+ achievements and a full evolution codex

PRESTIGE FOR THE LONG HAUL
Rebirth for Stardust and permanent multipliers. Ascend for Aether Cores, the Aether element, and game-changing perks. True offline progression keeps your nest working for up to 48 hours.

FAIR & FRIENDLY
No pay-to-win. Every slime and evolution is earned through play. Optional rewarded ads and a one-time Remove Ads purchase are the only monetization. Light theme by default, dark mode available, big readable text, and a UI that respects your phone's navigation bar.

A game by F7 Developer. Crafted for players worldwide.
